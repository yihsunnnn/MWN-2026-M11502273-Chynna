# Study Note: 5 Tips in 5 Minutes - Presentations

### Main Idea

The video explains five simple ways to create clear, attractive, and effective presentation slides. The main purpose of slides is to support the speaker and help the audience understand the message, rather than showing all of the information on the screen.

### 1. Keep It Simple

Slides should be simple and easy to understand. Avoid complicated templates, unnecessary decorations, and too much information. If a slide becomes crowded, it is better to divide the information into several slides. A simple design helps the audience focus on the main point.

### 2. Never Read from Your Slides

The speaker should not put everything they want to say on the slides and then read it aloud. Too much text distracts the audience because they have to choose between reading the slide and listening to the speaker. Therefore, slides should contain only important keywords or short phrases.

### 3. Use Effective Visuals

Pictures and other visual elements can make a presentation more interesting and memorable. A good image can communicate emotion and meaning more effectively than a large amount of text. However, the visuals should be related to the topic and should help explain the message.

### 4. Keep Data Simple

Charts, graphs, and numerical data should also be easy to understand. Avoid showing too many numbers or complicated graphs at the same time. The important information should be clear, accurate, and readable even from a distance.

### 5. Use Repetition and Consistency

A presentation should have a consistent visual style. Use the same fonts, layouts, and a simple color palette throughout the slides. Repetition creates unity and makes the presentation look more professional.

### Conclusion

The most important lesson is that **slides should support the presentation, not become the presentation itself**. Simple slides, fewer words, meaningful visuals, clear data, and consistent design allow the audience to focus on the speaker and understand the main message more easily.
