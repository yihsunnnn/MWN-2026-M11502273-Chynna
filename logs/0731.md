# Experiment 07：CPU Mean vs Max Result

| Dataset | CPU temperature method | Input feature count | Samples | Train | Validation | Test | MAE | RMSE | Max Error | Inference time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| cpu_mean | Mean of CPU sensors | 12 | 2252 | 1576 | 338 | 338 | 0.624°C | 0.903°C | 3.977°C | 0.000032 sec |
| cpu_max | Max of CPU sensors | 12 | 2252 | 1576 | 338 | 338 | 0.730°C | 0.999°C | 4.125°C | 0.000047 sec |

# Result Summary

| Comparison | Better method | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| MAE | cpu_mean | Mean version has lower average prediction error |
| RMSE | cpu_mean | Mean version has lower overall error |
| Max Error | cpu_mean | Mean version has lower maximum error |
| Stability | cpu_mean | Mean temperature is smoother and easier to predict |
| Hotspot sensitivity | cpu_max | Max temperature is more sensitive to high-temperature CPU sensors |
